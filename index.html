About me

A Product Manager who can build, not just brief.

As a Product Manager at Rosier Foods, I lead the strategy, design, and execution of digital products that enhance customer experience and drive business growth. I collaborate closely with cross-functional teams—including engineering, design, marketing, and operations—to transform business objectives into scalable, user-centric solutions.

My experience spans product strategy, roadmap planning, UX optimization, stakeholder management, and agile product development. With a strong foundation in Electrical & Computer Engineering from REVA University and a background in UI/UX design, I bring a unique blend of technical expertise, design thinking, and business acumen to every product I build. I enjoy solving complex problems, making data-informed decisions, and delivering products that create measurable value for both users and the business.
